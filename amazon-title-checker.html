2026 Amazon Title Rules (Updated July 2026 COSMO Update)

Amazon tightened title rules in July 2026 as part of the COSMO ranking update. Here's what changed:

75 char max No subjective claims Brand first Key product attributes
  • Max 75 characters — Titles beyond this are truncated in search (COSMO update, July 2026). Affects ~900K+ sellers.
  • Subjective claims banned — "best", "best seller", "top rated", "free", "guaranteed", "perfect" can get listing suppressed.
  • Brand must be first word — If your brand exists, it goes at the start.
  • Keywords still matter — COSMO understands search intent, not just keywords. Place the main search term naturally.
  • All Caps only for acronyms — "USA" ok, "BEST" not.
  • Promotional text banned — "Free shipping", "Sale", "50% off" = suppressed.
