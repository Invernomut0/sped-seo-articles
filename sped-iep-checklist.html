📋 The 6 Mandatory IEP Sections (Quick Checklist)

Section 1: Present Levels of Academic Achievement and Functional Performance (PLOP)

    ✓What it is: Where the student is RIGHT NOW academically and functionally ✓Required data: Latest evaluation results, classroom observations, teacher reports, standardized test scores, parent concerns ✓Common mistake: Copy-pasting from last year's IEP without updating data points

Section 2: Measurable Annual Goals

    ✓What it is: What the student will achieve in the next 12 months ✓Formula: [Student] will [behavior] when [condition] to [criterion/standard] ✓Required: At least one academic goal + one functional goal (state-dependent) ✓Must be: Measurable, observable, time-bound

Section 3: Special Education and Related Services

    ✓What it is: Specific services the student receives (pull-out, push-in, therapy) ✓Must specify: Service type, frequency (minutes/week), location, start/end dates ✓Related services: Speech, OT, PT, counseling, transportation as needed

Section 4: Accommodations and Modifications

    ✓Accommodations: HOW the student learns (extended time, preferential seating, audiobooks) ✓Modifications: WHAT the student learns (reduced assignments, alternate curriculum) ✓Required: Must be linked to specific goals and PLOP data

Section 5: Transition Plan (Age 14-16+, state-dependent)

    ✓Post-secondary goals: Education/training, employment, independent living (as appropriate) ✓Required transition services: Courses of study, experiences, related activities ✓Skills checklist: Self-determination, daily living, financial literacy (state-dependent)

Section 6: Progress Monitoring & Reporting

    ✓How often: At least as often as report cards (state-dependent) ✓Progress measures: Data collection methods for each goal ✓Reporting format: Parent communication method (notebook, email, conference)

💡 Quick Tips for Faster IEP Writing

    📌Template first: Start with your state's IEP template. Only fill in the blanks. 📌PLOP → Goals → Services: Follow the cascade. Don't write services before goals. 📌Use data, not opinions: "The student seems to struggle" → "The student scored 65th percentile on {assessment}". 📌Parent input isn't optional: Document their concerns verbatim in the PLOP. 📌Compliance check at the end: Review the 6-section checklist before signing.
