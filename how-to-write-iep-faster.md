## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Before the workflow, it helps to know what's actually available in 2026:

**Enterprise district tools** (Playground IEP, IEP Smart, Goalbook Toolkit) cost $50-200 per month *per district*. They're powerful but inaccessible to individual teachers, substitutes, or parents advocating for their child.

**Single-feature AI tools** (goal generators, PLOP writers) exist as Chrome extensions or web apps. They handle one section at a time. You still copy-paste between tools, format everything manually, and cross-check compliance yourself.

**General AI chatbots** (ChatGPT, Claude) can be prompted to help, but without a system prompt and structured workflow, the output drifts from compliance requirements and district-specific formatting.

## The 3-Phase AI IEP Workflow

Here's the system I use for every IEP now. It follows the actual IEP document structure:

### Phase 1: Present Levels (PLOP) — 15 minutes

The PLOP is the foundation. Everything else derives from it.

**Step 1:** Paste the relevant portions of the psychoeducational evaluation, recent report cards, teacher observations, and any functional behavioral assessment into your AI tool.

**Step 2:** Use a structured prompt that asks for:
- A clear statement of the disability category
- How the disability affects classroom performance (specific subjects, skills)
- Strengths (at least 2-3)
- Parent concerns (from the meeting notes)
- Data points (recent grades, benchmark scores, observation notes)

**Key technique:** Don't ask the AI to "write the PLOP." Ask it to "analyze these evaluation data points and draft a Present Levels of Academic Achievement and Functional Performance statement in the standard IEP narrative format, organized by domain (academic, social-emotional, behavioral, physical as applicable), flagging any claims that lack direct data support."

This forces the AI to be specific and attribute claims to sources — which makes review faster.

**Step 3:** Edit for district-specific language. Most districts have preferred phrasing for certain language. This is where your expertise matters most. The AI gives you 80%; you refine to 100%.

### Phase 2: Measurable Goals — 20 minutes

PLOP done. Now the goals flow directly from it.

**Step 1:** For each identified area of need in the PLOP, ask the AI to generate 2-3 SMART goals. Specify:
- The student's current baseline (pull from your PLOP)
- The target performance level (be specific: "from 3/10 to 8/10" not "improve significantly")
- The measurement method (probe, rubric, work sample, observation count)
- The review timeframe (quarterly, bimonthly — match your district)

**Step 2:** The AI generates goals. You check each one against your district's SMART goal rubric. Some will need adjustment on the baseline numbers. That's fine — this takes 5 minutes per goal vs. 30-45 minutes of staring at a blank page.

**Step 3:** Add **objectives or benchmarks** if your district or the student's IEP requires them. These break each goal into 3-4 smaller, measurable steps.

### Phase 3: Services, Compliance & Packaging — 25 minutes

**Services & Accommodations:** Ask the AI to generate recommended services and accommodations based on the PLOP and goals. The AI should cross-reference the student's disability category with commonly matched services. You approve or adjust.

**Compliance checklist — the part most AI tools skip:**
- **IDEA:** Is the statement of special education services tied directly to the student's identified needs?
- **FAPE:** Are the services specific enough (frequency, duration, location) to provide a free appropriate public education?
- **LRE:** Is the placement recommendation justified by the PLOP and goals? Does it include the rationale for any time outside the general education classroom?
- **Transition planning** (if age 16+ or as required by state): Postsecondary goals and transition services.
- **Parent concerns:** Are all parent concerns from the meeting addressed in the document?

**No existing off-the-shelf tool runs this compliance checklist automatically.** This is where a prompt-based system with a built-in compliance checker (like the one I built) saves you from the most common rejection reasons from administrators.

**Final packaging:** Once all sections are drafted, the AI can help you:
- Ensure consistent terminology across all sections
- Check that every goal links back to a stated need in the PLOP
- Verify that services match the intensity of needs described
- Produce a clean, formatted document ready for your district's template

## Privacy and FERPA Compliance — Non-Negotiable

Before using any AI tool with student data:

1. **Remove all identifying information.** No names, student IDs, or school identifiers. Use "Student A" or "the student."
2. **Check your district's AI policy.** Some districts have explicit rules; others don't. When in doubt, use local processing tools or enterprise-approved platforms.
3. **Never paste protected health information** beyond what's necessary for the IEP itself (diagnoses from evaluation reports are generally acceptable; home life details are not).
4. **Use a FERPA-safe system prompt.** A well-crafted system prompt that instructs the AI to treat all output as confidential student data adds a layer of procedural compliance.

TCEA published a detailed guide on FERPA-safe AI workflows for IEPs in March 2026 — worth reading regardless of which tools you choose.

## Getting Started: A Starter Checklist

1. **Choose your AI tool.** This can be ChatGPT, Claude, or a specialized SPED AI tool. The workflow is tool-agnostic.
2. **Create a master prompt template** with system instructions for "expert special education consultant specializing in IDEA-compliant IEP development."
3. **Build your template documents.** Have evaluation summary, goal area list, and district format ready.
4. **Practice with a past IEP** (one you've already submitted and approved) to calibrate the AI's output quality.
5. **Scale to active IEPs** once comfortable.
